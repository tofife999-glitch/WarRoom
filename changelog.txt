War Room - changelog

0.14.0 (2026-10-22)
- Sounds: back to the game's own, one for switching tabs and one for opening a war report; War Room's made
  sounds are gone.
- The intercept line holds the event it shows while the pointer is on it, so a click opens that event's war.
- One switch for all screen touches: "warRoom_flourishes": false in settings.json turns every one of them off.

0.13.2 (2026-10-22)
- The Archive's sound: a relay, then a memory drum spinning up and settling.

0.13.1 (2026-10-22)
- The tab sounds as picked: Hostilities a heavy relay and a muffled buzz, Archive drive-seek chatter,
  Standings a teleprinter's keystrokes, Briefing a split-flap board.

0.13.0 (2026-10-22)
- The tab sounds now play (0.12.0's were mono files, which the game does not play as interface sounds), and
  they are new: the noises of the tablet itself (a relay and a radio channel opening, drive-seek chatter, rows
  ticking onto the display, a document drawn up), not tones. Loudness is "volume" in data/config/sounds.json.
- New wars: the stamp is gone; the card says "New conflict - started 21 days ago" in small print inside its
  amber frame.
- More screen touches, each switchable: a faction eliminated in a war is struck through on that war's row in
  the Archive timeline; a faction's tooltip gives its place in the Sector by colony size ("2nd of 14"); the
  relay line names the relay you are linked through ("Relay link steady: Askonia relay").

0.12.0 (2026-10-22)
- Each tab has its own short sound, made for War Room; opening a Faction or Alliance Archive plays the
  Archive's. Any of them can be set to another sound in settings.json.
- Alliance wars show who is gaining ground too ("Past 90 days: ..."), with the momentum arrow.
- New wars: a war's card keeps an amber frame and a "New war - day 12 of 30" stamp for its first 30 days (the
  frame still lights up once when Hostilities opens).
- The intercept line: "INTERCEPT" is no longer cut off, each event stays on one line, and clicking the line
  opens the report of the war it is about.
- More screen touches, each switchable: a trace of the Sector's tension over the past 3 cycles at the right of
  the tabs, and the title flickering on like a projection when War Room opens.
- The cycle bands on the timeline are much fainter.
- The opening sweep and the new-war flash run when a page opens or War Room comes back on screen, no longer on
  a click after a pause.

0.11.0 (2026-10-22)
- "Past 90 days" instead of "Past 30 days": who is gaining ground on each war, and the summary under the
  title, now look back 90 days. Set "warRoom_recentDays" in settings.json for another span, such as 180.
  Quiet still means under 10 points per 30 days.
- The "Engaged" light now marks wars with fighting in the last 30 days (was 3).
- More screen touches, each switchable in settings.json: an arrow before "Past 90 days" pointing to the side
  gaining ground, on war cards and reports; the card of a war begun in the last 30 days flashes once when
  Hostilities opens; the Chronicle's months as date stamps; and how far each system on a war report's
  Theatre line is from your fleet ("Askonia (14 ly)").

0.10.0 (2026-10-22)
- Standings for alliances: switch between Factions and Alliances. Each alliance shows its members, their
  colonies together, the change since records began, colony size taken and lost while in the alliance and
  its fronts under way; a row opens the Alliance Archive.
- More screen touches, each switchable in settings.json: an intercept line at the top of Hostilities (the
  latest events fading by), an "Engaged" light on cards of wars with fighting in the last 3 days, quarter
  marks on the advantage bars, today's line and faint cycle bands on the timeline, rank numbers, struck-through
  names of factions without colonies and a Sector total row in Standings, and quiet interface sounds when you
  switch tabs or open a war report.
- Removed: the scanlines and the RESTRICTED tag; the date, addressee and relay light now sit at the far right.
- "End of assessment" is no longer cut short.

0.9.0 (2026-10-22)
- Screen touches, each with its own switch in data/config/settings.json ("warRoom_flourish..."):
  - the report number after the source line ("No. 206-306": cycle and day);
  - "Prepared for Captain <your name>" under the date;
  - a relay light that pulses green while you are in comm relay range ("Relay link steady"), amber when not;
  - a small "RESTRICTED" tag at the top right;
  - faint scanlines behind the header;
  - a thin line that runs once along the header when a page opens;
  - "Compiled aboard <your flagship>, fleet intelligence section." and "End of assessment" at the bottom of
    each tab.
- The header is 4 px taller; the tabs now always sit on its bottom row.

0.8.0 (2026-10-22)
- Turning points: the war log notes when a side takes the advantage, or when the two are evenly matched
  again (with a margin, so a war near the line doesn't fill the log). They also show in the Chronicle.
- Big fights Nexerelin settles far from you (200 fleet points or more lost) now go into the war log as
  major battles, marked reported, with the system they were fought in; they also show on the timeline.
- War reports name the theatre: the systems where the war's logged events happened, busiest first.
- Hovering a timeline icon is more reliable: the hover area now covers the whole icon.

0.7.0 (2026-10-21)
- Hostilities: sort buttons on each section's heading (hourglass: war age, swords: battles, flag: colonies
  captured, scales: advantage). One click sorts most first, two least first, three back to the usual order;
  a small arrow shows which.
- Standings: click a column heading to sort by it the same way (names A to Z, then Z to A).

0.6.1 (2026-10-21)
- A war between two alliances that later merged now counts once in the merged alliance's service record.
- A Faction or Alliance Archive whose wars are all older than the Archive keeps says so, instead of "no
  wars on record" under a service record that counts them.
- Standings: in a save from before 0.6, the tooltip now says when War Room first noted a faction's colony
  size, instead of claiming that was when it first held colonies.

0.6.0 (2026-10-21)
- Service record at the top of every Faction and Alliance Archive: wars on record, under way, how they
  ended (ceasefires, peace treaties, enemies eliminated...) and who held the advantage at the end, then
  battles, colonies, ships, raids and invasions against its enemies.
- New Standings tab (after Archive): every faction holding colonies, by colony size, with the change since
  records began, colony size taken and lost in war, and wars under way. Click a faction for its archive.
- Wars the Archive no longer keeps still count in service records and standings.
- Demo: "warroom_demo big" makes a ten-cycle history with the Archive full, and the demo line shows how
  long each page took to build.

0.5.1 (2026-10-21)
- A faction's flag or name on the war cards and war reports opens its Faction Archive; an alliance's name
  (and each member's crest) in an alliance war opens the new Alliance Archive: the wars its members fought in
  the alliance, and its news. Opened from the report, these archives have a Back button.
- The Archive's bars have one shade for an advantage (the "clear advantage" shade is gone).

0.5.0 (2026-10-21)
- Faction Archive: the Intel screen's Factions tab has a small "Faction Archive (War Room)" button for the
  faction shown. It opens the Archive for that faction only: its wars, one row per enemy, and its events
  and news in the Chronicle. "All factions" goes back to the whole Archive.
- Ceasefire or peace treaty: wars that end from now on say which one ended them (in the war log, the
  Chronicle and the ended-war cards). Peace by other means, and older wars, still read "Hostilities ceased".
- Your own raids and tactical bombardments now count as raids won for the faction you fight for, with
  their own lines in the war log.
- The Archive legend explains all four bar shades: nothing to report yet, evenly matched, advantage,
  clear advantage.
- The report's source line no longer mentions projections.

0.4.2 (2026-10-21)
- Archive: no more dark box behind each war's icons and bar; the faint outline now sits right around
  the coloured bar only. Rows and links still light up when you hover them.

0.4.1 (2026-10-21)
- The Archive redesigned. Timeline: taller rows with each faction's flag, thicker bars with a faint
  outline, no blue row background, cycle gridlines, and War Room's own icons sitting on the bars in the
  colour of the side that scored (colony captured, major battle won, bombardment, ceasefire); a legend
  explains the icons and the bar colours. Hover an icon for its event.
- New Chronicle below the timeline: every notable event of every war and the Sector news, newest first
  by month, with filters and a link to each war's report. It replaces the Ended wars and Sector news lists.

0.4.0 (2026-10-21)
- Reads like an intelligence report on your ship: new wording throughout (war log, military report,
  "holds the advantage", "Past 30 days: ... gaining ground", confirmed and reported battles), a source
  line and an "As of" date under the title, and a sources note under each military report.
- Three tabs: Hostilities (the wars under way), Archive (a timeline of every war on record with
  colonies captured, major battles and ceasefires marked, plus ended wars and Sector news) and Briefing
  (how war weariness, peace, alliances, fleet and ground pools, raids, invasions and the war score work).
- Bombardments now say what happened to the colony: "Colony size reduced from 5 to 4." or "Colony
  destroyed."
- Battles won on a war's report shows how many of them were reported from far away.

0.3.1 (2026-10-20)
- Demo mode for trying the pages out: with Console Commands, "warroom_demo" fills War Room with a made-up
  history built from the sector's real factions, colonies and alliances (an alliance war, single wars of
  every kind, ended wars). Your own records are set aside and tracking pauses meanwhile; nothing of the
  demo is saved. "warroom_demo off" or loading a save brings them back; "warroom_demo new" makes another.

0.3.0 (2026-10-20)
- New design: the matrix and the tabs are gone. War Room now opens on Active wars: one card per war
  (your wars first, then the others, then wars that just ended) with a bar showing who is winning, who
  is gaining lately, battles won, colonies taken, ships lost and the latest event.
- Alliances at war are shown as one block with combined strength and totals, their fronts below.
- Each war has its own page: its story, what is happening now, the totals, strength now and the lead
  over time, with Back / Prev / Next and jumps to faction profiles, raid intel and the map.
- Now also tracked: off-screen fights (as estimates, marked "~"), bombardments, invasions beaten back,
  alliance changes and factions losing their last colony.
- Pirate wars are hidden unless you are in them; one click shows them.
- The probe page from 0.2.1 is removed. Saves from 0.2 are carried over.
- Settings: war score weights added; a "large battle" is now 200 fleet points.

0.2.1 (2026-10-20)
- Temporary: a "War Room UI probe" entry next to War Room, with four quick checks for the next version.
  It changes nothing in the game and goes away again in 0.3.

0.2.0 (2026-10-20)
- Records the wars from now on: when each began and ended, battles and who won them, ships and fleet
  points lost, colonies captured, Nexerelin raids and invasions (launched / succeeded, counting those
  being prepared or under way) and ground battles.
- Wars tab shows that history; hover a war for the full breakdown, click it for its log entries.
- New War log tab: wars, peace (with the war's totals), captured colonies, large battles (100 fleet points
  lost or more), alliance changes, factions losing their last colony; filters by kind; keeps the last 300
  entries. Both numbers are settings in data/config/settings.json.
- Still removable from saves: the history is saved as plain text values only.

0.1.0 (2026-10-20)
- First version, for Starsector 0.98a-RC8 with Nexerelin 0.12.2d.
- War Room intel entry under its own tag, with three tabs built from the current state: the war matrix,
  the ongoing wars and the factions.
- Can be added to and removed from running saves: nothing in the save needs the mod to load.
